STRATMAP Three-Seat Simulation

stratmap.sfai.fi · live simulation · Monday 9 Nov 2026

Three people, one operation, start to finish

Nothing is prepared in advance. You sit down, open the system cold and run one STRATMAP LITE operation through all four phases, the board package and the monthly HORIZON cycle. Each step names who acts, what to click, and what everyone should see. Tick steps as you go; ticks and notes stay in this browser.

TommyAdmin. Creates the operation, invites people, starts the process, keeps it moving.
NasirMember of the company: CEO (Accountable Executive). Answers the questions, uploads a document, corrects the plan, reports the month.
KimmoMember of the company: Board. The final decision maker. Decides every phase, reads the board package, approves the plan.
TommyNasirKimmoEveryoneWait 0 / 0 steps
BEFORE YOU START

Five minutes in the room

5 min

Do this together at the table. Nothing here needs the evening before.

LaptopsThree laptops, one per person, each in its own browser, nobody logged in. Everyone opens https://stratmap.sfai.fi.
E-mailInvitations arrive by e-mail. Nasir and Kimmo each need an address they can read right now that has no STRATMAP account yet. Nasir's nasir@sfai.fi and Kimmo's admin login already have accounts, so use another address (a personal one is fine). Tommy also needs a readable address.
PhoneTommy needs an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password). Admin logins ask for a one-time code. Nasir and Kimmo will be asked for one too, but only when they log in a second time.
NasirYour own admin password and authenticator for nasir@sfai.fi. You use it twice: to create Tommy's account (Part 1) and for the support test (Part 9). Do that in a private window so it does not mix with your member login.
One fileAny PDF or CSV on Nasir's laptop that looks like a budget or company document. One page is enough. The system scans it for malware before it is stored.
OptionalA fourth e-mail address (any of you with a second address) for the "outside reader" test in Part 9.
PART 1

Tommy gets an admin account

5 min

Nasir, as system owner, creates the admin. Tommy logs in for the first time and sets up his one-time code.

  1. 1NasirOpen a private window, go to https://stratmap.sfai.fi, log in as nasir@sfai.fi with your password and authenticator code.
  2. 2NasirGo to https://stratmap.sfai.fi/admin/users and click Add user. Name Tommy, Tommy's e-mail, role Admin, status Approved, and a password of at least 12 characters. Save. Tell Tommy the password.
    Look atTommy's row appears in the user list with role Admin.
  3. 3TommyOn your own laptop, go to https://stratmap.sfai.fi and log in with that e-mail and password. The system asks you to set up a one-time code: scan the QR code with your authenticator app and type the 6-digit code.
    Look atYou land on Operations. Top right shows your name. A card New operation is on the right. If the screen is in Finnish, switch the top-bar language to English.
  4. 4NasirClose the private window. You are a company member from now on, not an admin, until Part 9.
PART 2

Tommy creates the operation

3 min

A fresh LITE operation. The first thing to notice: the process refuses to start until someone is named as the final decision maker.

  1. 5TommyOn Operations, in the New operation card: name Demo Oy growth plan 2027-2029, Delivery profile = STRATMAP LITE, Content language = English. Click Create.
    Look atYou land on Organisation. Four phase boxes: 0 Set-up and decision rights in progress, 1 Starting point, 2 Plan, 3 Living plan not started. A warning reads "Name the final decision maker (for example the board) before the process can continue."
  2. 6TommyTry to click Start the starting-point questionnaire now, before anyone is invited.
    Expected refusal"Name the final decision maker first (for example the board). The process cannot continue without one." This is on purpose: phase 0 cannot end unless it is clear who decides.
PART 3

Nasir and Kimmo join the company

8 min

Tommy invites both. Each accepts the e-mail, sets a password, signs the NDA and confirms their role.

  1. 7TommyOn Organisation, Add participant: Nasir's fresh e-mail, name Nasir, role Accountable Executive / planning sponsor (the dropdown's default). Add.
    Look atA green note: "Invitation e-mailed to …". If instead an orange note shows an invitation link, e-mail is off: copy the link and send it to Nasir by chat.
  2. 8TommyAdd participant again: Kimmo's fresh e-mail, name Kimmo, change the role to Board. Add.
  3. 9TommyClick Save planning organisation.
    Look atThe "Name the final decision maker" warning is gone. Both people are listed, roles not yet confirmed.
  4. 10NasirOpen the invitation e-mail, click the link. Type a password twice (12+ characters), tick the NDA and terms box, activate.
    Look at"Welcome! Your account is now active." You are logged in and see Operations with Demo Oy growth plan 2027-2029 listed. The link works once only.
  5. 11KimmoSame: open your invitation e-mail, set a password, tick the NDA box, activate.
  6. 12NasirOpen the operation. On Organisation, click Confirm my role.
  7. 13KimmoOpen the operation. On Organisation, click Confirm my role.
    Look atIn Tommy's window (it refreshes itself) both roles now show as confirmed. Tommy, Nasir and Kimmo all see the same four phases. Nobody sees an "Add participant" box except Tommy.
PART 4

Phase 1: the starting point

12 min

Tommy opens the questionnaire. Nasir, as CEO, tells the company's story and uploads one document.

  1. 14TommyOn Organisation, click Start the starting-point questionnaire. Leave "Business ID (optional)" empty.
    Look atPhase 0 done, Phase 1 Starting point in progress. A link Answer the questions appears.
  2. 15NasirOpen Workshops (or click Answer the questions). Answer at least the mandatory questions 1, 2, 3, 4 and 9 with one made-up small-company story. Example: 14 staff, revenue 2.1 M€, cash 180 k€, growth goal: double revenue by 2029, one investment decision: a new production line for 400 k€. Part B is the 12-month cash forecast; fill it if quick, or leave it empty on purpose. Click Save answers.
    Look atSaved without error. Empty Part B answers will later show up in the board package as decision risks. That is a test, not a bug.
  3. 16NasirOpen Documents, upload your PDF or CSV, visibility All participants.
    Look atThe file appears in the list after the malware scan. Kimmo refreshes his Documents page and sees the same file. If you see "The malware scanner is not available", note it and continue without the file.
  4. 17EveryoneGo back to Organisation.
    Look atPhase 1 now says waiting for a decision. Kimmo's window shows decision buttons. Nasir's and Tommy's windows show "The phase is waiting for the final decision maker." and no buttons.
PART 5

Kimmo decides phase 1

5 min

The board reads the starting-point package and lets the next phase start. Only the board can.

  1. 18KimmoAt phase 1, open Board one-pager and Decision paper.
    Look atThe confidence badge shows B (the company's own answers). The decision paper lists Nasir's answers and the open items. Everything is in English because the operation's content language is English.
  2. 19NasirTry to find a way to accept phase 1 in your own window.
    Expected refusalThere is no button for you. The server refuses too: a CEO cannot take the board's decision.
  3. 20KimmoChoose Accept – the next phase starts, reason The starting point is right, click Record the decision.
    Look atPhase 1 done, Phase 2 Plan in progress. Under the phases a note says "The system is drafting the plan." No one pressed any further button.
PART 6

The automatic run and the correction round

15 min, mostly waiting

The system drafts, checks, red-teams and revises the plan on its own. Then it hands the plan to the CEO for corrections, and after that to the board.

Wait 5 to 10 minutes. All three pages refresh themselves every 10 seconds. The note under the phases changes in this order: 1. The system is drafting the plan. 2. The system is checking the sources. 3. The red team is reading the plan. 4. The system is revising the plan. 5. Your turn: read the plan and record corrections.
  1. 21EveryoneWhile waiting, look around. Kimmo: open Documents and confirm you see Nasir's file. Tommy: scroll to the section Readers outside the planning group and read what it offers. Nasir: open Workshops again and check your answers are still there.
  2. 22TommyOnly if the note says "The automatic run stopped at …" with a reason: click Resume automatically. The step is retried once and the run continues. Note the reason in the box below.
  3. 23NasirWhen the note says "Your turn", the section Correction round appears in your window. Write one factual correction, for example We have 14 staff, not 12, and click Send feedback. (The other choice is Nothing to correct – continue.)
    Look atThe note changes to "The system is revising the plan" and the checks run again. Kimmo and Tommy have no correction box: this round belongs to the company's executive.
  4. 24Wait3 to 5 minutes more.
    Look atPhase 2 says "The plan is ready for the board to decide." Kimmo's window shows the section The board's decision · version 1.
PART 7

The board package and the approval

12 min

Kimmo reads what a board actually gets: a one-pager, a decision paper with sources and confidence, and the full package. Then he approves.

  1. 25KimmoIn The board's decision · version 1, look next to the buttons.
    Look atA confidence badge with a letter, for example "◕ B · Good: probable (60–85 %)", a line "Decision risk: …" and counts per class A/B/C/D. Expect B, C or D. The rule beside it: the decision's confidence is the weakest class among the claims it relies on. If you left Part B empty, the missing cash forecast is one of the decision risks.
  2. 26KimmoOpen Board one-pager. Press Cmd+P or Ctrl+P to see the print preview, then cancel.
    Look atOne page: the question, the proposal, the confidence, cash low points and the cash floor, top risks, what happens next. It fits one printed page.
  3. 27KimmoOpen Decision paper and read down to Full analysis.
    Look at"What STRATMAP did" step by step, the options, red team objections with answers, the corrections (Nasir's "14 staff" is here), open items, and Claims and sources: every claim with its A–D chip, its sources and their quality.
  4. 28TommyOpen Board package and Monitoring annex. In the annex, find the plan's cash trigger (something like "Cash < 170") and write it in the notes box below. You need it in Part 8. Also check which monthly offers go to the CEO and which to the board.
    Look atAt the end, "Identifiers in this document" names every trigger, decision point and measure in words. No codes like DR-2 anywhere in the text.
  5. 29NasirTry to approve the plan in your own window.
    Expected refusalNo approve button. "The phase is waiting for the final decision maker."
  6. 30KimmoOptional, costs 5 minutes of waiting: choose Return for revision, reason Calculate cash without the subsidy, Record the decision. The revision and the checks run by themselves and the board gets version 2. Skip if time is short.
  7. 31KimmoChoose Approve the plan, reason Board 9 Nov 2026, click Record the decision.
    Look atPhase 2 done, Phase 3 Living plan in progress. The daily web check is switched on automatically. The section HORIZON-LITE – monthly monitoring appears in everyone's window.
PART 8

HORIZON: the first month

10 min

The company reports a month where cash is below the plan's trigger. The system reacts, offers decisions to the right person, and records what they decide.

  1. 32NasirIn HORIZON-LITE – monthly monitoring, open The month's report (the company's own standard report). Report date 2027-01-31. In Measures, enter a cash value below the trigger Tommy wrote down in step 28. Click Record the report.
  2. 33WaitAbout 1 minute. No button to press.
    Look atThe month gets a review, "What you decide this month…", and one or more decision offers. Each offer says who may decide it: the CEO or the board. HORIZON runs when something changes, not on a timer.
  3. 34NasirOn the cash offer, click Do not act on this now, reason Salaries were deliberately paid early, click Record: not acting now. If the offer is routed to the board, Kimmo does this step instead.
    PointThis is a recorded decision with a reason and a version, not a dismissed alert.
  4. 35KimmoDecide the remaining offers that are marked for the board. Nasir decides any that are marked for the CEO.
    Look atWhen every offer is decided, the month shows "Recorded in the plan" on its own.
  5. 36NasirOptional: record a second month (2027-02-28) with the same cash value, then a third with a lower one.
    Look atSame value: the trigger shows "Deliberately not acted on" and stays quiet. Lower value: "alerts again: the situation got worse" and a new offer appears.
PART 9

Outside reader and SFAi support

8 min

Who else can see the plan, and how every view is logged. The board grants access; an admin must give a reason to open a customer's operation.

  1. 37KimmoIn Readers outside the planning group, click Let an outside party read. E-mail: the fourth address. What they may read: phase packages and the living plan. Days: 90. Reason: Funding application, bank X. Click Allow reading.
  2. 38EveryoneWhoever owns that address: open the e-mail link in a private window, tick the NDA box, set a password. Then go to https://stratmap.sfai.fi/app/ops.
    Look atA section Shared with you. The funder sees a read-only page with the packages and the latest months. Nothing else opens: no Organisation, no Documents, no decisions.
  3. 39KimmoOpen View and access log. Then click End access.
    Look atThe funder's views are listed with time. After "End access", the funder's private window loses the operation on refresh.
  4. 40NasirOpen a private window again and log in as nasir@sfai.fi (an admin who did not create this operation). Open Demo Oy growth plan 2027-2029.
    Look atYou are stopped by "Open the operation for support". Write the reason Support ticket #1: demo (at least 10 characters) and click Open for support. Only then does the operation open.
  5. 41KimmoRefresh View and access log.
    Look atThe support access is in the same log, with Nasir's reason and a limit of at most 8 hours. The customer always sees when SFAi looked in and why.
PART 10

Wrap up

5 min

Two last checks, then decide whether to keep the operation.

  1. 42KimmoLog out and log in again with your member account.
    Look atYou are asked to set up a one-time code with your authenticator app. People who hold a deciding role must use one from their second login on. Nasir gets the same when he logs in again.
  2. 43EveryoneSwitch the top-bar language to Finnish in one window and back.
    Look atScreens follow each person's own choice. The AI-written plan stays in English, the operation's content language.
  3. 44TommyOnly if you want to remove the test data: on Organisation, open the collapsed panel Closing the operation and data retention, click Erase the operation now (demo or finished), paste the exact name Demo Oy growth plan 2027-2029, click Erase permanently. Otherwise keep it: it is a complete worked case.
